Assignment 13 - Tic Tac Toe

Two players, one board. Line up three runes or three arrows to win.

ᚱ Wizard 0
Draws 0
➶ Ranger 0

Wizard’s turn · ᚱ

How to Play

Share one device. The wizard places runes (ᚱ), and the ranger places arrows (➶). Take turns choosing an empty square. Get three of your pieces in a row, column, or diagonal to win. If the board fills up before either player wins, the round ends in a draw. Choose Play again to start the next round. Players alternate who goes first.
