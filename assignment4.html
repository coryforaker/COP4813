RC Circuit Calculator

This calculator uses the capacitor charging equation to calculate and plot capacitor voltage over time. Since I am currently taking Advanced Electrical Circuits with Professor Pallais, it only seemed fitting.

Calculator Inputs

Equation: Vc(t) = Vs(1 - e-t/RC)

Time Constant: 1.00 seconds

Calculated Values

Time (seconds) Capacitor Voltage (V)
