BSIT Course Advisor

Record completed courses, check prerequisites, and build a plan for one upcoming semester.

This classroom planner covers selected programming and database courses from DSC's 2026–2027 BSIT program. It is not a complete degree audit. Admission, transfer evaluations, and other degree requirements are outside its scope. Offering terms are catalog-listed patterns, not live section availability. View the BSIT program guide .

1. Courses already completed

Check courses you have passed with a C or better. Foundation courses are listed for recording prior credit only; this tool does not plan their enrollment.

2. Review enrollment options

Drag an available course into the plan, or use its Add to Plan button. Planned courses do not count as completed prerequisites for this same semester.

Select completed courses to review your options.

3. Your semester plan

Drop available courses inside this gold border.

    Notes and selections remain in this page while it is open. Download your plan before refreshing or leaving.
